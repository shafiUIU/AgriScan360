I checked your code. Since you are **NOT using Bosch AI Studio**, your BME688 is basically acting as a **raw environmental + gas resistance sensor**.

From the code, the sensor only provides:

* Temperature (°C)
* Humidity (%RH)
* Pressure (hPa)
* Gas Resistance (Ohms / kOhms)

It does **NOT** provide:

* IAQ score
* CO₂ equivalent
* VOC concentration in ppm
* Gas type (ethylene, ammonia, alcohol, etc.)
* Mold detection

The code calculates rot suspicion only from the change in gas resistance (ΔkOhms).

# I would additionally save

From all collected gas readings:

### gas_min_kohms

Lowest resistance observed.

### gas_max_kohms

Highest resistance observed.

### gas_mean_kohms

Average resistance.

### gas_std_kohms

Standard deviation.

### sample_count

How many gas samples collected during the 16-image scan.

---

# Why?

Suppose two tomatoes:

| Tomato | Delta |
| ------ | ----- |
| A      | 10    |
| B      | 10    |

Both have same delta.

But:

| Tomato | Std Dev |
| ------ | ------- |
| A      | 0.5     |
| B      | 8.0     |

Tomato B may be releasing VOCs much more inconsistently.

The ML model can learn this.
